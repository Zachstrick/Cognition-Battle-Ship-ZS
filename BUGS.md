# Battleship debugging notes

| Bug found | Fix | How it was verified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting with the keyboard could focus an off-screen target. | Let the browser scroll the first target into view. [Fix](https://github.com/Zachstrick/Cognition-Battle-Ship-ZS/pull/1) | At 1000 × 400 in Chrome, Enter started the game and focused visible A1; another Enter fired. |
| Forced colors made board states indistinguishable. | Added distinct glyphs for ships, misses, hits, and sunk ships. [Fix](https://github.com/Zachstrick/Cognition-Battle-Ship-ZS/pull/1) | In Chrome forced-colors emulation, a sunk Destroyer showed ⊗ on both squares. |
| Shot effects were missing in forced colors and Reduce Motion. An initial fix still replaced them with a plain fade in Reduce Motion. | Kept the expanding explosion and splash in all modes and used system colors for the high-contrast shapes. [Visibility fix](https://github.com/Zachstrick/Cognition-Battle-Ship-ZS/pull/3) · [Full animations](https://github.com/Zachstrick/Cognition-Battle-Ship-ZS/pull/4) | Chrome played full hit and miss effects with Reduce Motion on; high-contrast shapes were checked separately. |
| Firing again could cut off the previous shot effect on that board. | Gave each shot square its own effect, cleared when its animation ends or the game resets. [Fix](https://github.com/Zachstrick/Cognition-Battle-Ship-ZS/pull/5) | Chrome gameplay checks covered overlapping effects and reset. |
| Larger browser text could push fleet labels outside the case and clip them. | Let fleet entries wrap and clipped only animations at the board boundary. [Fix](https://github.com/Zachstrick/Cognition-Battle-Ship-ZS/pull/5) | Code review confirmed that fleet labels could wrap and their container no longer clipped them. |
| The red hit pegs looked orange. | Adjusted the tray's tint without changing the cream miss pegs. [Fix](https://github.com/Zachstrick/Cognition-Battle-Ship-ZS/pull/6) | A fresh Chrome load showed coral-red hit pegs and cream miss pegs. |
| Short desktop windows cut off the lower board, and “Submarine” broke mid-word. | Tightened case spacing, moved controls beside the case on wide, short screens, and widened the fleet rail. [Fix](https://github.com/Zachstrick/Cognition-Battle-Ship-ZS/pull/7) | Both boards fit at 1440 × 780 and 1366 × 768 in Chrome; “Submarine” stayed on one line. |
| On short landscape screens, focusing the first result button could scroll past the end animation. | Focused the report label first and put the artwork beside the buttons on short landscape screens. [Fix](https://github.com/Zachstrick/Cognition-Battle-Ship-ZS/pull/8) | At 667 × 235 in Chrome, both scenes opened in view and the first Tab reached Review board. |
| Longer turn messages could push the board down on phones. | Kept the controls on their own row and reserved two lines for the status message. | Chrome layout checks at 320–600 px confirmed the board stayed put as turn messages changed. |

Run `node --test` to verify fleet placement, AI targeting, hidden ships, turn order, duplicate shots, and both match outcomes. Browser regression checks also covered the mobile layout and resetting during a pending AI move.
